Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Decode ("the App," "we," "us") is a content scheduling and publishing tool that helps a video creator manage and publish content to TikTok using TikTok's official APIs. This Privacy Policy explains what information Decode accesses through a connected TikTok account, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account to Decode through TikTok Login Kit, we request only the following:

  • user.info.basic — your TikTok display name, avatar image, and open ID, used solely to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload / video.publish — the video files, captions, and publishing settings you provide through Decode, used solely to upload and publish that content to your connected TikTok account.

We do not access your TikTok followers, direct messages, or any content other than what you explicitly provide for publishing.

How we use this information

Information accessed through TikTok's API is used exclusively to confirm which TikTok account is connected, to upload and publish the content you provide at the time and in the manner you configure, and to keep a record of what was published and when, so you can review your own publishing history within Decode. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the publishing feature you've requested.

Data storage and security

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored securely and used only to make the API calls described above. We retain account connection data only for as long as your TikTok account remains connected to Decode, or as required to provide the service.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok account information with third parties. Information is transmitted only to TikTok's own servers, as required to operate the publishing feature.

Your choices

You can disconnect Decode from your TikTok account at any time from within the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Manage apps. To request deletion of any data Decode holds about your account, contact us using the information below.

Children's privacy

Decode is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact us
