Publishing pipeline

Plan the video once.
Decode carries it to TikTok.

Decode is a scheduling and publishing tool for video creators. Connect a TikTok account once, queue up finished content, and Decode publishes it through TikTok's own API — on the schedule you set.

What it does

One queue. One connected account.

Decode doesn't manage a feed of strangers' accounts — it manages a publishing queue for the TikTok account that connects and authorizes it.

Connect securely
Sign-in happens through TikTok Login Kit. Decode only reads the basic profile info needed to confirm which account is connected.
Queue your content
Finished video files, captions, and a publish time are added to a simple queue. No editing happens inside TikTok itself.
Publish on schedule
When the scheduled time arrives, Decode calls TikTok's Content Posting API to upload and publish the video to the connected account.
For reviewers

How the TikTok integration works

Decode requests the smallest set of TikTok permissions needed to do the two things above: confirm an account, and publish to it.

user.info.basic
Confirm the connected account
Reads display name, avatar, and open ID so Decode can show which TikTok account is currently connected. Not used for any other purpose.
video.publish
Publish directly
Publishes the queued video straight to the connected account at the scheduled time, without a separate manual step in the TikTok app.
